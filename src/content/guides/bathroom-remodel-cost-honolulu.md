There is no current published average for a bathroom remodel in Honolulu alone. The closest published data is a 2025 regional average of $27,710 for a midrange bath remodel and $86,863 for an upscale one. [1] This guide gives the published numbers with their year, explains what moves the price in Honolulu, and describes what the permit and licensing rules say. It is not legal advice. All sources were checked on November 22, 2026.

## How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Honolulu?

The 2025 Cost vs. Value Report from the Journal of Light Construction lists these average job costs. [1]

| Project | Pacific region, 2025 | National, 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bath remodel, midrange | $27,710 | $26,138 |
| Bath remodel, universal design | $45,921 | $42,183 |
| Bath remodel, upscale | $86,863 | $81,612 |
| Bathroom addition, midrange | $65,326 | $60,645 |
| Bathroom addition, upscale | $121,772 | $111,255 |

Honolulu is not among the Pacific region cities listed in the 2025 report, so these are regional averages and not Honolulu prices. [1] The national column matches the report's national averages page. [13]

The last Honolulu figures we found in the same report series are from 2021: $28,685 for a midrange bath remodel, $44,259 for universal design and $87,670 for upscale. [2] In that 2021 data, Honolulu's midrange figure was about 3 percent above the Pacific region average of $27,883 and about 17 percent above the national average of $24,424. [2] The percentages are our own arithmetic, and 2021 figures are too old to use as a current price.

We found no published source that puts a number on Honolulu bathroom remodel costs for 2026, so we do not give one. A written bid for your own bathroom is the only reliable number.

## Why would Honolulu cost more than a regional average?

Published sources point to three things.

- **Labor and location.** A 2026 Hawaii construction cost report says labor constraints persist and that Hawaii's isolation keeps baseline construction costs high compared with other North American markets. [3]
- **Rising costs.** The same firm's cost index for Honolulu rose 5.93 percent from April 2025 to April 2026, the highest annual growth among the metros in its report. [4] That index covers construction in general, not bathroom remodels.
- **General excise tax.** Hawaii's general excise tax is 4 percent, Honolulu adds a 0.5 percent county surcharge, and the maximum rate a business may pass on to a customer is 4.712 percent. [5]

## What drives the cost of a bathroom remodel?

**Scope and finish level.** In the 2025 regional data, the upscale bath remodel cost a little more than three times the midrange one. [1]

**Moving plumbing or wiring.** In Honolulu a permit is required for electrical or plumbing work that is not exempted, and that work must be done by licensed trades. [6] [7]

**Adding a bathroom instead of remodeling one.** The 2025 regional average for a midrange bathroom addition was $65,326, more than double the midrange remodel. [1] Honolulu requires a building permit for additions, and the number of bathrooms allowed in a dwelling is set by a chart in the Land Use Ordinance. [7]

**The age of the home.** Under the federal lead rule, anyone paid to do work that disturbs paint in housing built before 1978 must be certified in lead-safe work practices. [8] The Environmental Protection Agency recommends that owners of homes built before 1978 hire a lead-safe certified contractor. [8]

**Permit and plan costs.** Honolulu permit fees are based on project value, meaning fair market labor and materials, and a plan review fee applies when plans must be submitted. [7]

**Surprises.** The state consumer agency advises setting a budget in advance and keeping a reserve for changes or unexpected costs. [9]

## Do I need a permit to remodel a bathroom in Honolulu?

The Honolulu Department of Planning and Permitting (DPP) says a building permit is required to alter, repair or improve a building, and for electrical or plumbing work that is not exempted. [6]

DPP lists these commonly applicable exemptions. [6]

- Painting, cabinet work, floor covering, and replacing windows or doors
- Maintenance repairs using the same or similar materials, up to $10,000 in value per 12-month period, that do not affect electrical or plumbing installations
- Electrical repair by a licensed electrical contractor, not involving service entrance equipment, up to $2,500 per 12-month period
- Plumbing repair by a licensed plumbing contractor, up to $2,500 per 12-month period

Even when no permit is required, DPP says all work must still comply with local laws and ordinances. [6]

Which side of the line your project falls on depends on its details, and the DPP page we read was last modified January 30, 2025. Check with DPP before you start.

Two other points from DPP's building permit FAQ. Emergency work may start without a permit, but you must apply for one by the next working day. [7] Processing time varies with plan clarity, project complexity, DPP's backlog and outside approvals. [7]

## Who is allowed to do the work?

- **Licensed contractor.** The Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA) says a contractor's license is required for any project over $1,500 in labor and material and for any project that needs a building, electrical or plumbing permit. [10]
- **Licensed electricians and plumbers.** DPP says all electrical and plumbing work must be done by contractors licensed by the State of Hawaii and that owners cannot do this work themselves. [7] DCCA says electrical and plumbing contractors must employ licensed electricians and plumbers to do the actual work. [10]
- **No handyman shortcut for plumbing or electrical.** DCCA says the handyman exemption for jobs under $1,500 does not cover electrical or plumbing work. [9]
- **Permits.** DCCA says only licensed persons or companies may apply for electrical or plumbing permits. [11]

## Does a bathroom remodel pay back at resale?

In the 2025 Cost vs. Value Report, the Pacific region midrange bath remodel recouped 91 percent of its cost at resale and the upscale remodel 44.5 percent. The national figures were 80 percent and 41.7 percent. [1] These are regional and national averages, not Honolulu results.

## How do I get an accurate price?

DCCA suggests getting at least three bids for the same work and materials and asking why if the amounts vary a lot. [9] State rules require a written home construction contract that states the contractor's license number, the start date and number of days to completion, the work and materials, and each subcontractor's name and license number. [12] A 2026 appeals ruling struck down the rule's separate requirement to state an exact dollar amount, because state lien law also allows cost plus contracts. [14] DCCA also advises paying as the work is completed and not paying everything up front. [9]

When you compare bids, check that each one covers the same things: demolition, plumbing, electrical, tile, fixtures, permits and tax.

## FAQ

### What is the average cost of a bathroom remodel in Honolulu?

No current Honolulu average is published. The 2025 Pacific region average was $27,710 for a midrange bath remodel and $86,863 for an upscale one. [1] The last Honolulu figure we found was $28,685 for a midrange remodel in 2021. [2]

### Do I need a permit to replace a vanity or a toilet?

DPP lists cabinet work as exempt, and plumbing repair by a licensed plumbing contractor up to $2,500 per 12-month period as exempt. [6] Confirm your specific job with DPP.

### Can I do my own plumbing or electrical work in my bathroom?

DPP says owners cannot do electrical or plumbing work themselves. It must be done by state-licensed contractors. [7]

### How much does it cost to add a bathroom?

The 2025 Pacific region average was $65,326 for a midrange bathroom addition and $121,772 for an upscale one. [1] No Honolulu figure is published for 2025.

### Is tax added to a remodel in Honolulu?

Hawaii has a general excise tax. On Oahu the maximum rate a business may pass on to a customer is 4.712 percent. [5]

### My house was built before 1978. Does that matter?

Yes. Paid work that disturbs paint in housing built before 1978 must be done by firms certified in lead-safe work practices. [8]

### How long does a bathroom remodel take?

We found no solid published timeline, so we do not give one. It depends on the scope and on whether a permit is needed. DPP says its processing time varies. [7]
