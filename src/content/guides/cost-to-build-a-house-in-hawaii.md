A 2026 construction cost report puts single-family home construction in Honolulu at $345 to $645 per square foot. [1] That range covers hard construction cost only. It is not a quote, and your lot, design and finishes decide where you land in it. This guide explains what the number includes, what it leaves out, and what moves a Hawaii build budget. Each figure is given with the year of its data. All sources were checked on November 22, 2026.

## How much does it cost per square foot to build a house in Hawaii?

The most current published figure we found comes from Rider Levett Bucknall (RLB), a construction cost consultancy with offices in Honolulu, Hilo, Kona and Maui. [2] Its second quarter 2026 report lists Honolulu single-family residential construction at $345 (low) to $645 (high) per square foot of gross floor area, with data current to the middle of that quarter. [1] The first quarter 2026 report gave the same range. [2]

Here is that range as simple arithmetic.

| Home size | At $345 per square foot | At $645 per square foot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,200 square feet | $414,000 | $774,000 |
| 1,500 square feet | $517,500 | $967,500 |
| 2,000 square feet | $690,000 | $1,290,000 |

The table is our own multiplication of the RLB range. It is an illustration, not a price for any home.

## What does that number include and leave out?

RLB describes its figures as estimates of hard construction costs, and says costs may vary with site conditions, climate, specification standards and market conditions. [1]

The report does not spell out what sits outside hard costs. In construction budgeting, land, design and engineering fees, permit fees and financing are usually counted separately as soft costs, so budget for those on top.

The RLB figures are for Honolulu. We found no solid published cost per square foot for Maui or Hawaii Island, so we do not give one.

## Are building costs in Hawaii still going up?

Yes, according to two published indexes.

- RLB's comparative cost index for Honolulu rose from 32,798 in April 2025 to 34,743 in April 2026, an increase of 5.93 percent and the highest annual growth among the metros in the report. [1]
- The State of Hawaii's Honolulu Construction Cost Index for a single-family residence rose 6.3 percent in the first quarter of 2026 compared with the first quarter of 2025. [3]

RLB's Hawaii commentary for the second quarter of 2026 says labor constraints persist and that Hawaii's isolation keeps baseline construction costs high compared with other North American markets. [4]

## What drives the cost of building a house in Hawaii?

**1. Size and specification.** Cost per square foot is multiplied by floor area, and RLB lists specification standards as one reason costs vary. [1]

**2. Your site.** RLB also lists site conditions. [1] In Honolulu, plans must be stamped by a licensed professional when the work is in a special management area, a flood hazard area or a special design district, and retaining walls 5 feet or higher must be stamped by an architect or engineer. [5]

**3. Design and engineering.** DPP says plans must be stamped and signed by a licensed professional when work on a one-story residence exceeds $180,000 or work on a two-story residence exceeds $162,000. [5]

**4. Permit fees.** Honolulu permit fees are based on project value, meaning fair market labor and materials. A plan review fee also applies, and each structure needs its own permit and fees. [6]

**5. Time.** Long permit waits add carrying cost. The UHERO housing factbook notes that the 30-year fixed mortgage rate reached 6.4 percent by the end of March 2026 and that higher rates raise borrowing costs. [7]

**6. General excise tax.** Hawaii's general excise tax is 4 percent, and all four counties add a 0.5 percent surcharge through December 31, 2030. The maximum rate a business may pass on to a customer is 4.712 percent. [8] Ask your contractor how the tax is shown in the contract price.

**7. Required features.** DPP says solar water heaters are required for new single-family dwellings. It also says homes generally need one parking space per 1,000 square feet and that paved areas cannot exceed 75 percent of the lot. [6]

**8. Labor and location.** RLB reports persistent labor constraints and high baseline costs tied to Hawaii's isolation. [4]

## How long does a building permit take in Hawaii?

The UHERO Hawaii Housing Factbook 2026 reports these processing times for single-family permits. [7]

| County | Days | Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hawaii County | 127 | Permits issued in 2025 |
| Maui County | 186 | Permits issued in 2025 |
| Kauai County | 309 | Permits issued in 2025 |
| City and County of Honolulu | 394 | Permits issued in the first half of 2025 |

The Honolulu figure predates the city's new permitting system, and later data was not available to the report's authors. [7] The same report says Hawaii's average permit processing time is more than three times the national average. [7] Processing times change. Check with your county permitting office before you set a schedule.

## Does the price of existing homes tell me what a new one costs?

Only as context. In 2025 the median single-family sale price was $1,175,000 in Maui County, $1,110,000 in Honolulu, $1,100,000 in Kauai County and $465,000 in Hawaii County. [7] Those are sale prices of existing homes, not construction costs.

## Can I build as an owner-builder?

State law has an owner-builder exemption from the contractor licensing law. It applies to owners or lessees who build or improve residential or farm buildings for their own use, or for use by grandparents, parents, siblings or children, and who do not offer the structure for sale or lease. [9] Selling or leasing within one year of completion is treated as evidence the structure was built for sale or lease. [9] The exemption does not cover electrical or plumbing work that must be done by licensed persons. [9] The owner must supervise the work, hire licensed subcontractors for licensed work, and carry workers' compensation insurance for unlicensed workers, who are treated as the owner's employees. [9] An owner-builder cannot recover from the Contractors Recovery Fund. [9] This is a description of the statute, not legal advice.

## How do I get a real number for my lot?

The Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA) suggests setting a budget with a reserve for changes, getting at least three bids for the same work and materials, and asking why if the amounts vary a lot. [10] State rules require a written home construction contract that states the start date and number of days to completion, the work and materials, and the share of work to be subcontracted with each subcontractor's name and license number. [11] A 2026 appeals ruling struck down the rule's separate requirement to state an exact dollar amount, because state lien law also allows cost plus contracts. [12]

## FAQ

### What is the average cost per square foot to build a house in Hawaii?

The most current published range we found is $345 to $645 per square foot for single-family construction in Honolulu, from RLB's second quarter 2026 report. [1]

### Does that cost include land?

RLB describes the figures as hard construction costs. [1] Land, design, permits and financing are usually budgeted on top.

### Why is building in Hawaii so expensive?

RLB points to persistent labor constraints and Hawaii's isolation. [4] RLB's Honolulu cost index rose 5.93 percent in the year to April 2026, the highest annual growth among the metros in its report. [1]

### How long does a building permit take?

For single-family permits, a 2026 UHERO report shows 127 days in Hawaii County, 186 in Maui County and 309 in Kauai County for 2025, and 394 days in Honolulu for the first half of 2025. [7]

### Is there sales tax on a new home build in Hawaii?

Hawaii has a general excise tax. The rate is 4 percent plus a 0.5 percent county surcharge, and the maximum pass-on rate is 4.712 percent. [8]

### Do I need an architect or engineer?

In Honolulu, stamped plans are required when residence work exceeds $180,000 for one story or $162,000 for two stories, and in flood hazard areas, special management areas and special design districts. [5]

### Is it cheaper to build on Hawaii Island than on Oahu?

We found no published cost per square foot for Hawaii Island. Hawaii County did have the shortest permit time and the lowest median sale price in the UHERO report. [7]
